THE POWER PROXY (LEVEL 10)

Lock the magnetic resistance dial permanently at Level 10. Because Force is constant, your RPM (Velocity) becomes a direct, mathematically perfect proxy for Watts.

THE RAMP TEST (UNBROKEN)

  • Stage 1 (Baseline): Pedal at exactly 40 RPM for 2 minutes. Record Heart Rate.
  • Stage 2 (Ramp): Increase to 50 RPM. Hold for 2 minutes. Record HR.
  • Stage 3+ (Climb): Continue increasing by 10 RPM every 2 minutes.

THE KILL-SWITCH MARKERS

The test ends immediately if any of the following biological or mechanical failures occur:

  • Plank / Posture Failure: Core collapses or spine sags.
  • Technique Degradation: Wild side-to-side hip shifting to force the pedals.
  • Heart Rate Skyrockets: The lactate threshold is breached violently.

Your MAP is the highest RPM sustained for 2 full minutes before these markers hit.

THE DISTANCE STANDARD

Men: 20-24kg. Women: 12-16kg. The bell must float to exactly chest height on every rep to ensure distance (Work) is constant. Use the built-in metronome for pace.

THE RAMP TEST (UNBROKEN)

  • Stage 1 (15 SPM): 1 swing every 4 sec. Swing for 2 mins. Record HR.
  • Stage 2 (20 SPM): 1 swing every 3 sec. Swing for 2 mins. Record HR.
  • Stage 3 (24 SPM): 1 swing every 2.5 sec. Swing for 2 mins. Record HR.
  • Stage 4 (30 SPM): 1 swing every 2 sec. Swing for 2 mins. Record HR.

THE KILL-SWITCH MARKERS

The test ends when technique degrades (using the back instead of the hips), grip fails, or you cannot keep pace with the metronome beep. Your MAP is the highest SPM sustained for 2 full minutes.
